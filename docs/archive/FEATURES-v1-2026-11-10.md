# Features considered

Every feature looked at, ranked. **Must** = version 1 is not usable without it. **Should** = planned, has a card, can slip. **Later** = worth doing once real use shows the need; no card yet. **Won't** = decided against, with the reason, so it is not re-argued.

"New" marks features the owner did not mention. Card numbers refer to `docs/cards/`.

## Capture

| Feature | Rank | Card | Reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One-action voice capture from a locked phone, with start and save tones | Must | 01, 12, 15 | The whole idea. If it takes an unlock and three taps, nobody will use it at the moment they put the glasses down. |
| Hands-free finish (stops when you stop talking) | Must | 12 | Hands are full when you are putting something away. |
| Vibration alongside the tones (new) | Must | 12 | Phones are often on silent; without it the user cannot tell whether it worked. |
| Undo on the capture screen for a few seconds (new) | Must | 12 | Recognition will sometimes produce nonsense; the cheapest fix is at the moment of capture. |
| Correct the last note later (edit text or fields) | Must | 18 | "Frank" vs "Frankie". Without correction, errors accumulate and trust goes. |
| "I moved it" / "actually…" understood as a correction of the last item (new detail) | Must | 10, 11 | People correct themselves by speaking again, not by opening an editor. |
| Notification action as a lock-screen trigger (new) | Must | 15 | The one trigger that Android's documentation supports on every phone. |
| Quick-settings tile, home widget, launcher shortcuts | Should | 15 | Nearly free; different people will prefer different ones. |
| Cover-screen widget for the flip phones | Should | 02, 15 | Two of three users have flips and will mostly have them closed. |
| Default-assistant role (side/power key long-press, headset long-press) | Should, per person | 16 | The only true one-button trigger found; costs that person Gemini on the long-press, so it is their choice (Q1). |
| Spoken read-back of what was saved (new) | Should | 27 | Catches recognition errors without looking. On for the parents; off by default otherwise (it says private things aloud). |
| "That sounded like a question" / "save that as a note?" when capture and ask are mixed up (new) | Should | 20 | People will press the wrong button; the app should not file a question as a note. |
| Mark a note private by saying "private note…" (new) | Must | 10, 21 | The only practical way to mark local-only at the moment of speaking. |
| Automatic private marking for passwords, PINs, codes (new) | Must | 10 | People will tell it the gate code. That must never go to a model. |
| Samsung side-key double-press, lock-screen shortcut, back-tap | Should, if spike 01 passes | 15 | Depends entirely on what the phones allow from a locked state. |
| Photo of the drawer or shelf attached to a note | Later | — | Useful ("which box?"), but needs the phone unlocked and looked at, the opposite of the core flow. Add as an optional step from the saved note. Photos would be on-device only and in the backup. |
| NFC "location stickers" (tap the tag in the frunk, then speak; the place is filled in) (new) | Later | — | NFC does not work on a locked phone, so it is not a trigger; it is a neat way to get indoor place precision. |
| Bluetooth "where did it disconnect" auto-notes for car and earbuds (new; seen in Memora) | Later | — | Answers "where did I park / leave my earbuds" with no speech at all. Needs Bluetooth permission and a broadcast receiver; modest. |
| Bluetooth headset media button | Won't (as a media key) | — | Media keys go to whichever app last played audio. The headset's assistant gesture works through card 16 instead. |
| Shake or chop gesture | Won't | — | Android forbids opening the microphone or an activity from a background sensor service; the workaround is a permanent microphone-type service that dies at reboot, plus false triggers and battery cost. Motorola's own chop cannot be reassigned. |
| Wake word ("Hey Stash") | Won't | — | Not available to third-party apps; a software version means the microphone is always on. |
| Continuous or ambient recording | Won't | — | Opposite of the privacy goal, and of the law in some places when other people are present. |
| Smartwatch tile (Galaxy Watch) (new) | Later | — | A wrist button would be the best trigger of all for someone who wears one. A separate Wear OS app; revisit if the owner or his wife wears a watch (Q13). |
| Typing a note | Must | 18 | Sometimes you cannot speak. A text box on the home screen is trivial. |

## Understanding

| Feature | Rank | Card | Reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Text, timestamp, location and structured fields per note | Must | 08, 11, 14 | Must-have 2. |
| Offline rules-based structuring (new) | Must | 10 | Makes the app work with no key, no network and for private notes; also the safety net when the model fails. |
| Hosted model behind a small interface, swappable | Should | 21 | Better on unusual phrasing and synonyms. "Should", not "must", because the app is designed to stand without it. |
| Dates resolved on the phone; the model is never told the date (new) | Must | 09, 21 | Keeps the "time never leaves the phone" rule intact without losing "on Jan 5th". |
| Item aliases generated at structuring time (new) | Should | 10, 21 | Gives synonym search ("specs") without an embedding model. |
| "Did you mean Frank?" for near-miss names; never merge silently (new) | Must | 11, 18 | The main defence against recognition errors on names. |
| Telling the user when the app is unsure ("Check these") | Must | 18 | A memory you cannot trust is worse than none. Low confidence is shown, not hidden. |
| Items with multiple copies ("spare key", "the blue hat") | Should | 09, 11 | Common enough for keys and glasses that latest-wins would give wrong answers. |
| Multilingual speech and notes | Later | — | Depends on the family's languages (Q2). The data model does not care; the speech model and the rules do. |
| On-phone language model instead of a hosted one | Later | — | Removes the key and the network. Gemini Nano support on these exact phones is unclear; revisit when it is. |
| Custom vocabulary / hot-words for the speech engine (family names) (new) | Later | — | If spike 03 shows names are the main error, and the chosen engine supports biasing, this is the targeted fix. |
| Voice-print to recognise the speaker | Won't | — | Not a security boundary, no platform API, fails with a cold, and the fingerprint already does the job. |

## Privacy, safety and upkeep

| Feature | Rank | Card | Reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Encrypted database, key in hardware-backed Keystore | Must | 08 | Must-have 6. |
| Reading gated by biometric or device credential; capture not | Must | 17 | Must-have 9. |
| Encrypted backup to a folder the user controls, with a recovery code | Must before family use | 25 | A lost phone must not lose years of notes; cloud sees only ciphertext. |
| Restore on a new or replaced phone | Must before family use | 25 | Same. |
| Export (readable list, spreadsheet, full data) and delete everything | Should | 26 | The user's data must be takeable and erasable. |
| Local-only notes never sent to a model, enforced by tests | Must (if a model is used) | 21 | Must-have 8. |
| Monthly spend guard on the model (new) | Should | 21 | Belt and braces with the provider-side cap. |
| "What is stored and what is sent" page in plain words (new) | Should | 26 | Trust for the non-technical users, and for the owner's own audit. |
| Health check that says when a permission or the trigger has stopped working (new) | Must before family use | 27 | Silent failure is the worst failure for the parents. |
| Sealed inbox so a locked phone cannot decrypt notes | Later (has a card) | 30 | Raises the bar from "app code refuses" to "the key is unavailable". Intricate; not needed against a person holding the phone. |
| Sharing between people (household shopping list) | Later, opt-in | — | Needs a transport, which means a server or a third-party service, against the brief. If wanted, the simple version is "share this list" through the normal Android share sheet to Messages; no sync (Q8). |
| Sync between one person's devices | Won't for now | — | Nobody has two phones in this plan; backup and restore covers replacement. |
| Telemetry, analytics, crash reporting | Won't | — | Constraint. A local, on-device "last error" page in settings is enough for the owner to debug. |
| Use Android's own cloud backup | Won't | — | Would copy an unreadable database to Google and give false comfort. |
